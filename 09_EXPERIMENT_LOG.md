# 9. Experiment Log

Chronological record of what was tried, what was measured, and what was
concluded — including the approaches that failed and why. Numbers are as
measured at the time.

---

## 1. Offline pipeline brought up

`run_perfume.py` built and ran over the 180-frame recorded sequence.

- CAD prepared by `prepare_mesh.py`: metric, base-centered.
- `register()` on frame 0 (the only annotated mask), `track_one()` after.
- **Result: 180/180 poses restored.** `check_iou.py`: IoU **0.40** on the
  annotated frame. `check_tracking.py`: mean depth agreement **81.2%**,
  minimum **67.9%**, **zero** frames below 60%.

This is the project's verified baseline. Everything else is measured against it.

> A 6-frame clobber of `debug_perfume/` during debugging had to be restored.
> `debug_perfume/` is regenerated by re-running the offline command, so this is
> recoverable, but do not hand-edit it.

## 2. `score_pose` added, and thresholds calibrated

The problem: a diverged pose is indistinguishable from a good one by eye until
it is far too late. Needed a ground-truth-free metric.

Added `score_pose()` — render the CAD at the pose, compare rendered depth to
measured depth within 10 mm over the rendered silhouette.

Calibrated over **all 180 frames** using their verified-good poses:

| Population | min | median | max |
|---|---|---|---|
| Good poses (n=180) | **43.7%** | **58.9%** | **87.3%** |
| Deliberately displaced pose | **0%** | — | — |

Decisions:
- `MIN_FIT = 0.30`, sitting inside the empty `0% … 43.7%` gap.
- **A 50% gate was rejected** — it would have falsely rejected **21 of 180**
  good frames. The good distribution is wide (43.7–87.3%), so the gate must
  respect its lower tail.
- `MIN_IOU = 0.25`, against the one annotated mask which scores 0.40 when the
  pose is correct.

Gate wired into `live_perfume.py` at registration and every 5th tracked frame.

## 3. First live D405 run diverged

`live_poses.jsonl`, 331 poses. The tracker flew off the bottle.

`live_poses_run2.jsonl`, 4416 poses, measured properly:

| Statistic | Value |
|---|---|
| Max translation step | **2171 mm** |
| Second largest | 865 mm |
| Max rotation step | **136.9°** |
| p50 / p90 / p99 rotation step | 5.6° / 17.5° / 48.7° |
| Frames with step > 40° | 87 |
| Trajectory extent at the end | x ∈ [−1604, 557], y ∈ [−510, 1221], z ∈ [−521, 824] mm |

**Conclusion:** the quality metric *does* detect the divergence, but detection
alone is useless — the bad pose is still logged, still drawn, and still used as
the next frame's seed, so the tracker walks away. A gate is a speedometer, not
a brake. Motion rejection is the top open item; the policy question (hold vs
auto-re-register) is unresolved.

Also measured here: registration succeeded on only 47% of attempts, and the IoU
sat at exactly the 0.25 gate — i.e. registration quality was marginal *before*
tracking even started.

## 4. Symmetry: an incorrect hypothesis, tested and removed

**Hypothesis:** the top-up/top-down flip is a symmetry, so poses could be
canonicalised to remove it.

**Tested it.** A 180° spin about the model's +Y (long) axis gives silhouette
IoU **0.9993** — that symmetry is real. But flips about the *other* axes are
not equivalent, and a "top-up" canonicalisation does not correspond to a spin
about the bottle's own axis at all.

**Conclusion:** the only genuine ambiguity is the 180° spin about the long
axis. The top-up canonicalisation code was **removed** — it was actively wrong.
`format_pose` now labels output `(mod 180 about long axis)` and additionally
reports `long_axis_cam`, which is unambiguous where Euler angles are not.

This also explains `dR = 177.97 deg` between frames in `test_e2e_init.py` with
`dt = 0.07 mm`: the silhouette is identical and the position is unchanged. It
is the symmetry, not a tracking failure. **Anyone comparing poses across frames
must canonicalise about the long axis first.**

`perfume_foundationpose/` (the parallel implementation with `--symmetry_y`)
cannot solve the top-up problem either, and was not merged.

## 6. Streaming built, and a colour bug found

- `/tmp/opencode/g1_stream.py` → `/home/unitree/g1_stream.py`, TCP 8766.
- `/tmp/opencode/live_g1.py` → local viewer, estimates pose, draws the overlay.

**Bug: the preview had red and blue swapped.** Cause: the streamer requests
`bgr8` (native), the viewer fed BGR straight into drawing helpers that assume
RGB (`is_input_rgb=True`). Fixed by converting BGR→RGB for the pipeline and
RGB→BGR only for `cv2.imshow`. **Pose input was never affected** — only the
preview.

**Bug: `ModuleNotFoundError: No module named 'Utils'`.** Cause: `Utils` imported
before `perfume_common`, which is what puts `FoundationPose/` on `sys.path`.
Fixed by import order.

Also checked and confirmed correct: depth is transmitted as **raw `uint16`** in
sensor units with the scale sent in the header. Had it been converted to metres
and cast to `uint16`, every value would truncate to 0 or 1.

## 7. Automatic initialisation: the approach that failed, and the one that worked

**Request:** stop asking for a bounding box on every run.

**Attempt 1 — depth-only detection. Failed, and not fixably so.**

`find_candidates()` + `auto_register()`: threshold depth to 0.12–1.20 m,
morphological-close, take connected components, keep those whose metric size
matches the CAD.

- First run: **0/180** detections. Investigating revealed the *test* was wrong —
  it used the scene's median depth as ground truth.
- Redone with correct pose-projected ground truth:

| Measurement | Value |
|---|---|
| Scene depth p2 – p98 | **0.18 – 0.26 m** |
| Bottle distance | 0.186 – 0.195 m |
| Background median | 0.20 – 0.23 m |
| Candidates found | none, in most frames |
| The one candidate found | wrong — 19 × 14 mm |

**Conclusion:** the whole scene spans ~4 cm. The bottle and its background are
tens of millimetres apart; thresholding cannot separate them. This is a
fundamental absence of signal, not a tuning problem. A trained detector or a
colour/texture feature would be needed — not started.

**Attempt 2 — cached registration. Worked.**

Store the accepted pose + mask + K in `init_state.npz`; on startup re-score the
saved pose and either reuse it or re-register from the saved mask.

Verified on the recorded sequence with **no box drawn**:

| Frame | Method | Result |
|---|---|---|
| 0, 5 | `cached pose` | fit 54%, 49% |
| 30, 90 | **`re-registered`** from cached mask | fit 60% / 69% |
| 150, 179 | `cached pose` | fit 74% |
| no bottle present | **refused** | fit 0% |

**Conclusion:** exploiting "the user already told us once" works; exploiting the
bottle's depth signature does not.

## 8. G1 registration attempt: a mask failure, and the guard it produced

The user drew a box. It was **rejected**: depth agreement 31%, **mask IoU
0.00**. Digging in:

- Log line: `mask 1601 px (11% of box), object at 392 mm`.
- So the box was ~121 × 121 px, and the mask was 11% of it at 392 mm.
- A 153 mm bottle at 392 mm spans **236 × 85 px** — it cannot fit in a 121 px
  box. At 1.25 m it spans 74 × 27 px, which fits easily.
- **Therefore GrabCut had latched onto something small and near — almost
  certainly the user's hand — not the bottle.**

GrabCut had returned a confident, plausible mask of the wrong object, and
`register()` had returned a confident, useless pose. Exactly the silent failure
this project exists to prevent.

**Fix:** the bottle's projected size is computable from the CAD and the mask's
own measured distance, so check it. `expected_bbox_px(dist, K)` uses the two
largest CAD dimensions; the mask's bbox must be ≥ 25% of that.

| Case | Ratio | Result |
|---|---|---|
| The actual failure (~40×40 px at 392 mm) | 0.17 | **rejected** (correct) |
| Real bottle, 200×60 px at 0.5 m | 0.90 | accepted (correct) |
| Real bottle, 90×30 px at 0.8 m | 0.72 | accepted (correct) |

Two bugs were found while implementing it:
- `cad_extents_mm()` initially returned `[0.0, 0.1, 0.2]` — the mesh is
  authored in **metres**; the ×1000 was missing, which silently made the guard
  inert.
- `mask_from_box()` had no `K` parameter, so `NameError` on the drawn-box path.
  Added `K=None`, and `_pick_and_guard` skips the size check when `K is None`
  rather than crashing, so a generic `mask_fn` still works.

Regression check after both fixes: `test_e2e_init.py` unchanged at
`dt = 0.07 mm`, and `test_cache.py` reproduces all six cached-init behaviours
plus the refusal.

## 9. Camera outage handling, and two protocol bugs

The D435i dropped at 1082 frames. The streamer survived (by design: it holds
the socket open and reopens the pipeline) but the **local viewer died**, then
spammed reconnects. Two distinct bugs:

1. **Header sent only once.** Guarded by `if not outage:`, so any client
   connecting after the first outage read frame bytes as a header:
   `UnicodeDecodeError: 'utf-8' codec can't decode byte 0x8f`. Fixed with a
   per-connection `sent_hdr` flag.
2. **Client abandoned a healthy session.** A 30 s socket timeout fired while
   the streamer was legitimately waiting for the camera. The client now treats
   `socket.timeout` as "camera is re-enumerating, keep waiting" (180 s) and only
   reconnects on a genuine `ConnectionError`/`OSError`.

Also learned: `pkill -f "g1_stream.py"` run inside an SSH one-liner **kills its
own shell**, because `pkill -f` matches the pattern against the `bash -c`
command line that is running it. The rest of the command silently never ran.
Kill by PID instead.

## 11. Open items, in priority order

1. **Motion rejection** in both live trackers (reject > 60 mm or > 25° per
   frame; hold the last good pose; do not log or re-seed). Policy question
   unresolved: prompt vs auto-re-register.
2. **Recalibrate `MIN_FIT` / `MIN_IOU` for the D435i.** The 10 mm tolerance was
   calibrated on a D405 at 0.19 m; at 1.2 m on the D435i it is a much stricter
   relative demand. This is a correctness issue for the G1 gates.
3. **Complete a successful G1 registration** so `init_state.npz` exists and
   auto-init is exercised on that path.
4. **Move the D435i to USB 3.x** and reduce Jetson load.
5. **Move `/tmp/opencode/` scripts into the project** — `live_g1.py`,
   `g1_stream.py` and all tests are one reboot from being lost.
6. Optionally: score every frame instead of every fifth, once the motion gate
   makes the cost worthwhile.
